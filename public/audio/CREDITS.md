# Audio credits

Everything here is CC0 (public domain dedication): no attribution is required, these are listed
so anyone can find the originals. What came from OpenGameArt.org was trimmed and converted. The
rest was made for this game by `scripts/make-music.mjs`, and is CC0 too.

## Sound effects

- `rifle`, `gun`, `turret`, `cannon`, `thump`, `blast`, `bigblast`, `collapse`, `hit`:
  "ImpactsGunSFX": https://opengameart.org/content/impactsgunsfx
- `heal`, `loot`: "Residue SFX": https://opengameart.org/content/residue-sfx

## Music

- `heroes` (the crew island and the hero screens): "I think I'd stay (jungle, chill)" by emmntt:
  https://opengameart.org/content/i-think-id-stay-jungle-chill
- `map`: made for this game from `heroes`, with a march and a double-time breakdown.
- `raid`: made for this game from `heroes`, sped up, and "Tense bass boost drum loop" by fupi,
  with most of its hi-hat taken out:
  https://opengameart.org/content/tense-bass-boost-drum-loop-140-bpm
- `scout`: made for this game from `heroes`, muffled, and a field drum cadence.
- `night`: "Massive Battle" by eldritch-grim: https://opengameart.org/content/massive-battle
- `win`: made for this game, a bugle and drum fanfare.
